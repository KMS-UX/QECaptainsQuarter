Quest reward summary (Quest & Mission §13).

```jsx
<QuestRewards xp={12450} reputation={450} currency={3200}
  items={[{rarity:'epic'},{rarity:'rare'}]}
  choice={[{rarity:'legendary'},{rarity:'epic'}]}/>
```

Omit any pill you don't need. `items` render in rarity-framed slots; `choice` renders a gold-glow "Choose One" row. Pass `image` on any reward to show real item art.
