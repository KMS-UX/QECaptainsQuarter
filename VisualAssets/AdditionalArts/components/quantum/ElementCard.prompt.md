Quantum element cards + field hazard badges (Starships, Quantum Field & Elements sheet).

```jsx
<ElementCard name="Neutronite" element="neutronite" rarity="Rare"
  source="Neutron Stars" usage="Armor, Hulls"
  description="Extremely dense and highly durable."/>

<HazardBadge level="danger" note="High energy fluctuations."/>
```

Seven elements (`quantium`→`aeon`), each with its own glow color from `--elem-*`. `HazardBadge` covers the 5 field risk levels (safe→unknown) with matching `--hazard-*` colors.
