Recipe — Hover: accent glow (--glow-teal) + --border-bright. Press/active: brighter fill (accent bg, --void-0 text) or gold underline for tabs, transition --dur-fast --ease-ui. Focus: always 2px solid var(--focus-ring) with offset — required for keyboard & controller. Disabled: --fg-disabled text, --border-panel, no glow, reduced opacity, no pointer events. Selection reuses the press recipe. Every interactive element must define all five.
