Quantum Effect: Captain's Quarters
Build Bible v1.0
Vision Statement

Quantum Effect: Captain's Quarters is a cozy sci-fi life simulation where the player lives aboard their own starship as its captain.
Rather than focusing on combat or grinding, the game encourages players to build a daily relationship with their ship, crew, AI companion, and the living galaxy.
The game evolves alongside the player's real life through time, weather, personal reflections, and dynamic events, creating the feeling that the ship continues its journey even when the player is away.

B. Opening Experience

First launch. Dark screen. Soft orchestral music. Stars slowly appear. A lone starship drifts through space. Title fades in. 
"QUANTUM EFFECT Captain's Quarters"

Button: Begin Journey

Player taps. Camera slowly approaches the Captain's door. Door slides open. The player walks inside in first-person. The room is quiet. The captain stands before the observation window. Outside—an endless galaxy. Only then does the interface appear. This becomes the player's first memory of the game.

C. Daily Flow

K. Future Expansion Compatibility

Captain's Quarters is the hub for future Quantum Effect experiences.

Potential expansions include:

Quantum Expeditions – exploration missions to unexplored systems.
Frontier Colonies – establish and manage settlements on distant worlds.
Fleet Command – oversee multiple ships and strategic operations.
Galactic Commerce – trading, manufacturing, and an evolving economy.
Quantum Legends – the original open-world RPG vision.

All future games can share the same save file and captain identity.

L. Technical Philosophy - not final, feasibility and achievability come first.
Engine: Not decided, please consider C# if applicable. Decide based on feasibility.
Architecture: Modular, event-driven, service-oriented, following the Quantum Engine roadmap established in the earlier prototype.
Platform: Mobile-first, with desktop support.
Session Length: 5–15 minutes.
Offline Support: Most simulation systems continue locally and reconcile when online features are available.
Save System: Versioned save files to ensure long-term compatibility as new modules are added.
